Lira Studio Official — SEO, AEO, GEO & e-commerce review

An outside-in assessment of lirastudioofficial.com — how search engines and AI engines see the store, how it performs as a Shopify e-commerce experience, and the highest-leverage fixes to grow discoverability and conversion.

Prepared by SIAR Digital Shopify Select Partner 22 June 2026

Lira Studio Official is a promising, on-brand Pakistani modest-pret womenswear store with real demand behind it — but it is leaving meaningful organic visibility and conversion on the table. Below: a scorecard, the findings by discipline, and a prioritised plan.

Method & scope. This audit was built outside-in — from what Google and AI engines have actually indexed about the store, the brand's external footprint, and Shopify's known platform behaviour — because direct page crawling was not available at the time of writing. That is the correct lens for SEO/AEO/GEO, but a handful of on-page technicals (Core Web Vitals, image alt text, full JSON-LD, canonical/hreflang, checkout flow) are marked VERIFY and should be confirmed with a direct crawl, which SIAR can run on request.

Good bones held back by a few high-impact gaps. The single biggest opportunity is AEO/GEO — a category almost no competitor in this market optimises for yet.

01 — SearchSEO analysis · 6.5/10

Strength

Strong, keyword-rich foundations

The homepage title — "Lira Studio Official: Solid Modest Pret dresses for Women online" — targets real intent. Collections are well-built and indexed with descriptive titles/descriptions, and products + prices are read by Google (Product structured data is present).

Critical

SKU-coded product titles waste your best SEO real estate

A large share of products are named ST-003, ST-009, ST-019, ST-020, ST-031, ST-032, ST-033… These titles carry no keywords and no search intent, weakening the product <title>, H1 and click-through. Rename every product descriptively, e.g. "Olive Karandi Embroidered Co-ord Set".

Critical

Price indexing anomaly

One product (ST-009) is indexed at "Rs.170,000" — almost certainly meant to be Rs.17,000. A single stray zero kills conversion and can poison feed/Merchant data. Audit all prices and the Product schema price field.

High

No content / blog layer

No editorial content is indexed, so the store is invisible for informational and styling long-tail ("how to style a kaftan for Eid", "linen vs crepe for summer pret") — content that also feeds AEO and GEO.

High VERIFY

International SEO unconfirmed

The store ships worldwide, but multi-currency, hreflang and market routing could not be confirmed. Without them, relevance and conversion leak for the large GCC/US/UK Pakistani-diaspora demand.

Medium VERIFY

Technical hygiene

Confirm Core Web Vitals, image alt text, and canonical handling (paginated ?page= collection URLs are indexed — watch for duplicate-content dilution).

02 — AnswersAEO analysis · 3.5/10

How well can answer engines (Google AI Overviews, voice, featured snippets) extract direct answers from the site? Currently, weakly — and this is a fast, first-mover win.

Critical

No FAQ page and no FAQPage schema

Answer engines reward concise, structured Q&A. The raw material exists (shipping times, customs, fabrics, fit) but it is scattered across product pages and the shipping page rather than structured for extraction.

High

Key buyer questions are unanswerable by an AI

"Do you ship to the UK/US?", "What's the return policy?", "How long is delivery?", "What size am I?", "How do I care for karandi/silk?" — precisely the questions AEO captures. Convert these into structured answer blocks.

High

No size guide

Sizing is implied only via model height on product pages. A measurement-based size guide answers fit questions and reduces returns — especially for international buyers.

03 — GenerativeGEO analysis · 3.0/10

How well do ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity understand and cite this brand? Currently poorly — with a real risk of misattribution.

Critical

Severe brand-name collision

"Lira" is crowded: Lira Clothing (US streetwear, at Pacsun/Tillys), Lira Studio BD (Bangladesh), Maya Pret "Lira", Verb "Lira Dress", LYRA Pakistan. An AI asked about "Lira Studio" can easily surface the wrong brand. Disambiguation is the #1 GEO job.

Critical

Weak brand entity

No evidence of a Knowledge Panel / Wikidata / strong Organization graph. GEO needs machine-readable identity: Organization & Brand schema with sameAs (Instagram, Facebook), consistent name/address/phone, and an authoritative About page stating plainly who/what/where.

High

Thin third-party citations & no on-site reviews

Generative engines favour brands corroborated across the web (press, listings, reviews, mentions). The footprint is mostly owned social, and no on-site review system was detected — little for an AI to cite.

Medium

Generic identity signals

A Gmail contact address and no physical address weaken the trust/entity graph that both buyers and AI engines read.

04 — StorefrontE-commerce QC · 6.0/10

Strength

Strong brand, merchandising & demand

Clear aesthetic and positioning, ~133K engaged followers, excellent collection taxonomy (fabric × occasion), active seasonal drops, and a smart "Immediate Delivery" segment separating ready-to-ship from made-to-order.

Critical VERIFY

Order flow may be semi-manual

The shipping page states orders dispatch "within 2 weeks after a sales advisor sends an order confirmation." If checkout isn't fully automated (a manual/WhatsApp confirmation step), it is a major CRO and scalability leak. Confirm that Shopify checkout completes the sale end-to-end.

Critical

No on-site reviews / ratings

No review app detected — no social proof on product pages, no rating rich snippets, weaker conversion and weaker AEO/GEO. Add Judge.me or Loox and seed from existing customers.

High VERIFY

Trust signals are thin

Gmail business email, no physical address, and a returns/exchange policy that isn't clearly published. For higher-value festive and international purchases, this suppresses conversion. Add a branded email, address, visible returns terms and trust badges.

High

Slow, customer-pays shipping

"All shipping costs are the customer's responsibility," ~2-week dispatch locally, 3–4 weeks international plus customs on the buyer. Acceptable for made-to-order, but set expectations clearly and consider free-shipping thresholds to lift AOV.

Medium VERIFY

Mobile UX, speed & payments

Confirm mobile experience, page speed and payment gateways (note the Pakistan Shopify third-party gateway surcharge). SKU-named products also hurt on-site search and recall.

Bottom line

A strong brand on a capable platform, under-optimised for how people now discover and trust stores.

Fix the SEO hygiene, structure the answers, build the brand entity, and tighten trust and order flow — and this store can convert materially more of the demand it already attracts.

What to doPrioritised action plan

Quick wins · 1–2 weeks
  1. Fix the ST-009 price and audit all prices & Product schema.
  2. Rename every SKU-coded product to descriptive, keyword-led titles with an SEO title template: {Product} {Fabric} {Occasion} – LIRA.
  3. Publish/verify a Returns & Exchange policy; add a branded email + address; install a reviews app and seed reviews.
  4. Add an FAQ page + FAQPage schema and a measurement-based Size Guide.
Strategic · 1–3 months
  1. GEO entity build: Organization/Brand schema with sameAs, an authoritative About page, consistent NAP, third-party citations/PR, Google Business & Wikidata.
  2. Content / blog engine for styling and fabric-care long-tail — feeding SEO, AEO and GEO at once.
  3. International SEO/CRO: multi-currency, hreflang, and clearer shipping/returns for diaspora markets (GCC, US, UK, Canada).
  4. Confirm & streamline the order/checkout flow for fully automated purchase, plus a CRO pass on the product page (sticky add-to-cart, trust badges, delivery-date clarity).
