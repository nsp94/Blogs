## 1. Strategy at a glance

SIAR Digital is a **Shopify Select Partner** specialising in **e-commerce builds engineered
for conversion (CRO)**. We sell three service lines across three client tiers in five markets:

| | Service line | Commercial form |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Web Design & Development | Project (milestone billing 50/40/10) |
| **B** | Web Operations & Maintenance | Monthly retainer (recurring) |
| **C** | On-Page SEO + AEO/GEO | Monthly retainer; AEO/GEO add-on + standalone |

**Three tiers, same names everywhere:** **Launch** (new/upcoming SMBs) · **Growth** (medium
business) · **Scale** (large business).

**Positioning:** offshore value with Select-Partner credibility — priced **above commodity
freelancers, below local incumbents** in export markets, and as the **premium local tier** in
Pakistan. We compete on CRO outcomes and a delivery process that protects the client's launch
date, not on being the cheapest.

**The two commercial bets that make the numbers work:**
1. **Every build converts into a retainer** via a bundle (Build + 3 months Ops + 3 months SEO).
   Retainers are the stability layer; builds are the upside.
2. **We monetise the #1 cause of delays** (late product info) with a paid *Content Concierge*
   add-on, while protecting our schedule with a content prerequisite + parking fee.

## 3. Tier definitions & capacity model

| Tier | Target client | Page templates | Build approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Launch** | New / upcoming SMB | up to 5 | Premium Shopify theme, configured + CRO essentials |
| **Growth** | Medium business | up to 9 (full capacity) | Custom theme, integrations, full CRO |
| **Scale** | Large business | 9+ (overflow / phased) | Advanced / headless / Shopify Plus, custom features |

**Capacity rule (the real constraint):** the Creatives DM delivers **9 pages / 18 designs
(desktop + mobile) per build cycle**. Launch ≈ ½ cycle, Growth ≈ 1 cycle, Scale ≈ 1 cycle +
overflow. **Realistic throughput ≈ 1–2 builds/month** while the rest of the team runs the
retainer book. This caps how many builds sales may promise concurrently — never book a third
concurrent build cycle without freelance creative overflow priced into the quote.

## 6. Delay-protection commercials (the core challenge)

The #1 cause of slippage is **late product information**. Baked into **every build SOW**:

1. **Product Information Pack (PIP)** — defined checklist that signals "content ready":
   product images, descriptions, SKUs/variants, prices, shipping/returns policy, brand assets,
   domain/DNS access. One source of truth, attached to the contract.
2. **Mobilization deposit — 50%, non-refundable** — books the production slot. (Then 40% at
   design sign-off, 10% at go-live.)
3. **Clock-start prerequisite** — the timeline and the committed go-live date **only begin once
   the PIP is delivered complete**. This removes our exposure to the client's content delays.
4. **PIP deadline + parking fee** — SOW sets a PIP submission deadline; **5 business-day grace**;
   thereafter a **weekly Parking Fee = 5% of build value/week** (or fixed: PK PKR 25k/wk; export
   $250/wk Launch, $500/wk Growth, $1,000/wk Scale) and the slot reschedules to the next free
   cycle. Protects cashflow and capacity.
5. **Phased Go-Live add-on** (rescue path) — launch with a seed product set / placeholders, then
   bulk-upload as a separate paid phase. Converts a stall into revenue instead of dead time.
6. **Content Concierge upsell** — our Products Manager + Creatives produce/clean the product
   content for a per-SKU fee. **Monetises the exact bottleneck that causes delays** — the
   single highest-leverage commercial move in this plan. Lead sales with: *"Don't have your
   product info ready? We'll build it for you — and your launch date stays put."*

## 7. Financial model — clearing PKR 2,500,000 / month (gross)

Two revenue streams: lumpy **project** cash (milestones) + stable **retainer MRR**. Headline:
**~1 export mid-tier build/month + a retainer book of ~10–12 clients clears the target.**

### 7.1 Cost-base sanity check (illustrative — replace with actuals)

Approx monthly: salaries ~PKR 1.18M (Sr Mgr 300k, Dev Mgr 250k, Creatives DM 180k, Sales 150k,
PM 150k, Products 120k, QA@20% 30k) + overhead/tools/office ~PKR 0.5M ≈ **~PKR 1.7M total**.
A **PKR 2.5M** gross target therefore covers costs with a **~PKR 0.7–0.8M monthly buffer** —
a sound cost-cover-plus-margin anchor.

### 7.2 Three scenarios (steady-state monthly)

**A — Local-heavy / early stage**
- PK builds: 1 Growth (500k) + 1 Launch (200k) = 700k
- PK retainers: 10 × ~80k = 800k
- 1 export Launch build, avg $3,500 = 973k
- + AEO/GEO & Concierge add-ons ≈ 50k → **≈ PKR 2.52M ✓**

**B — Balanced (target operating model)**
- 1 export Growth build/mo, avg $5,500 = 1,529k
- Retainer book 12 clients, blended ~$280 = 934k
- 1 PK Launch build + add-ons = 150k → **≈ PKR 2.61M ✓**

**C — Export-heavy / mature (headroom)**
- Export builds ~$9,000/mo = 2,502k
- Retainer book 15 × ~$300 = 1,251k → **≈ PKR 3.75M** (growth beyond target)

### 7.3 Ramp plan

- **Months 1–3:** attach an Ops + SEO retainer to every past/existing build (fastest MRR);
  list & pitch via the Shopify Partner Directory; land 1–2 export Launch/Growth builds.
- **Months 4–6:** reach Scenario B; introduce Content Concierge as default upsell; begin AEO/GEO
  as the wedge offer in GCC/PK/MX where no competitor prices it.
- **FX sensitivity:** a 10% PKR depreciation *raises* PKR revenue from USD retainers/builds —
  tailwind for the target. Hold USD pricing; review the PKR price card quarterly.

## 8. Objection handling (quick reference)

- *"Freelancers are cheaper."* → Select Partner, CRO-built, fixed launch date, one accountable
  team. We quote outcomes, not hours.
- *"Why a deposit before you start?"* → It books your dedicated production cycle and our
  creative capacity; it's how we guarantee your timeline.
- *"We're still gathering product info."* → That's exactly why the clock starts at PIP — your
  date is protected. And our Content Concierge can build the content for you.
- *"What's AEO/GEO?"* → How you get cited inside AI answers (ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Overviews).
  No one in your market prices it yet — being early is the advantage.
